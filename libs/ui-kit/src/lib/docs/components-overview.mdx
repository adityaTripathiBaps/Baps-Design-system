# Components

Every component on one page. Each card shows a still of the component; hover it (or tab to it) and the live story takes its place. The card also shows the component's name, how many examples it has, and **Copy prompt**. Click the card to open the component's page.

Cards follow the toolbar: the selected design system, light or dark mode, and the brand filter (no comparison stories, no stories pinned to the other brand). The stills are the Playwright visual baselines, which are captured in MyBKY light, so in another brand or in dark mode a card shows its name until you hover it. New components appear here without editing this page.
